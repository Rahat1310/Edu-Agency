## Destination and university cards

- Replace blanket medical accreditation and guaranteed “free with CSC” claims with specific eligibility and award conditions. A [World Directory listing](https://search.wdoms.org/) does not establish accreditation or endorsement.
- Remove undated QS/global-engineering rankings from China university cards.
- Differentiate X1 study longer than 180 days from X2 study of no more than 180 days. Do not require JW201/JW202 universally for both. Follow the [Chinese Embassy in Bangladesh checklist](https://bd.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/sghd/202509/t20250912_11707745.htm). X1 entrants must apply for a residence permit within 30 days.
- Add a bilingual China landing section, university comparisons, official sources, cost planning, scholarship guidance and practical questions.
- Correct university-card navigation so selecting a university retains that university filter through pagination and comparisons.
- Include NENU alongside the four degree-focused universities, with its own official admissions link. All five China university cards now open their university-filtered listings; study-level links open undergraduate, master's and language listings.
- Label China fees as annual planning estimates in cards, details and comparisons. Clarify that intake months are course start dates, not application deadlines. Replace the promise of an exact scholarship waiver with profile assessment and university award decisions.
- Route ZJU postgraduate applicants to graduate information rather than the undergraduate guide. Remove blanket verified/accredited claims from the shared directory description while exact course titles remain under review.

## Applying catalog corrections

`npx tsx --env-file=.env.local scripts/correct-china-programs.ts` previews targeted changes.

Adding `--apply` first saves all affected original rows to a private JSON backup in the system temporary directory, then updates only those rows, checking their old content to avoid overwriting concurrent staff edits. IDs, publication status and student references are retained. The same corrections are included in future seeds; do not run the general seeder to correct an existing database.

Public Next.js program caches normally revalidate within one hour. A fresh deployment or the existing admin program-save action also refreshes the relevant pages.
