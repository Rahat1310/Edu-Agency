BUDGET + RANKING UNIVERSITY GUIDE FOR BANGLADESHI STUDENTS
Malaysia | China | South Korea | India
Client-facing shortlist with internal verification notes
Updated: September 2026

IMPORTANT USE NOTE
All figures below are planning ranges, not quotations. Tuition, intake dates, English scores,
foreign-national eligibility, programme availability, scholarship coverage and licensing rules
can change. Confirm every case from the official university and scholarship call before issuing
a client offer, invoice, or visa advice.

HOW TO POSITION THIS GUIDE
A. Prestige + value: ranked public universities and national institutions.
B. Practical value: good outcomes, lower entry barriers, more flexible intakes.
C. Scholarship-first: students with stronger grades, documents, and timelines.

MALAYSIA
================================================================================
Best pitch: English-medium study, multiple intakes, relatively simple transition from HSC/A-Level.
Public-university applications are selective; private universities usually offer more flexible
pathways. Tuition excludes accommodation, insurance, visa charges and living costs.

1) UNIVERSITI MALAYA (UM), Kuala Lumpur
Ranking/value angle: Malaysia's flagship public university; premium public-university target.
Best programs:
- Bachelor: Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Electrical Engineering,
  Business Administration, Economics, selected health-science pathways.
- Master/PhD: Computer Science, AI/data-related research, Engineering, Business, Public Health.
Planning tuition: approximately MYR 20,000-35,000/year for many non-medical bachelor's routes;
verify programme-specific international fee schedules. Some programmes quote a total-programme fee.
Intakes: commonly September; selected programmes may have additional intakes.
Entry hook: Strong HSC/A-Level profile; programme prerequisites apply. IELTS/TOEFL or an approved
English-medium route may be required depending on faculty.
Scholarship hook: Ask about UM merit awards and Malaysia International Scholarship routes for
eligible postgraduate applicants.
Agency curiosity line: "A flagship Malaysian degree can be more attainable than students think—if
we match their subject prerequisites and intake timing correctly."

CNY 3,000 for Master's and CNY 3,500 for PhD, but award details and eligible programmes must be
confirmed from the official annual call.

tuition support, medical insurance and a monthly allowance. Benefits, quotas, designated universities,
eligibility and deadlines change by annual call. Applicants should choose and follow the applicable
Embassy Track or University Track instructions for that cycle; do not present it as guaranteed.

INTERNAL CHECKLIST BEFORE CLIENT COUNSELING
================================================================================
1. Confirm institution recognition and programme accreditation.
2. Obtain the exact official international tuition schedule for the intended intake.
3. Separate tuition from application fee, visa fee, insurance, hostel, meals, deposits and flights.
4. Confirm whether the programme is English-taught, bilingual or local-language taught.
5. Confirm HSC/A-Level subject prerequisites and minimum academic score.
6. Confirm IELTS/TOEFL/PTE/Duolingo acceptance and any English-medium waiver policy in writing.
7. Check scholarship availability, deadline, coverage, renewal conditions and competition level.
8. For Medicine/Health: verify Bangladesh licensing, regulator requirements and internship pathway.
9. For India: verify foreign-national admission category, fee schedule and entrance-test requirements.
10. Give the student a written budget range and a clear disclaimer before taking service fees.
